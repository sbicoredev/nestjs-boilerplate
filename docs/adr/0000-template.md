# ADR NNNN: <short, descriptive title>

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem are we solving? What constraints (technical, team, timeline)
shaped the decision? Enough for someone with no memory of the discussion
to understand _why this came up_.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly in one or two sentences. Not a summary of
options considered — the actual choice.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it forecloses.
Include the honest downsides, not just the upsides — a decision with no
tradeoffs usually means the tradeoffs weren't looked for.

## Revisit when

Concrete signal that should trigger reopening this decision (a scale
threshold, a new requirement, a dependency going unmaintained) — not
"never" and not "whenever."
